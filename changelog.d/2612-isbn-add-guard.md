### Fixed
- **Add Book no longer takes a fallback provider's record while the primary is down** (#2612): a book picked from a result with no author id, such as a DNB result, has its author found by ISBN across the metadata providers. When the primary provider timed out, that lookup quietly moved on to the next provider and the book and its author were added with that provider's ids, which stuck and later produced a duplicate author once the primary was back. The add is now refused with a message saying the primary metadata provider did not answer and to try again shortly. A primary that answers and simply has no record for the ISBN still lets the other provider's record through, as before. This is the same guard the CSV, Readarr and Goodreads imports gained in #2332.
